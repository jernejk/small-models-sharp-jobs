# Recovery card

After five minutes of setup, pair with a working participant, open the next matching `workshop/0N-*`
snapshot, or use `facilitator/reference/solution/` as the finished reference. Without a model, run
the deterministic Gather acceptance commands from `workshop/03-gather`. A compatible hosted endpoint
is participant-owned and optional; use placeholders only and never share a facilitator key.

The corpus is a de-identified, attributed Victorian crash subset for analyst training, not claims automation.
