# Hybrid delivery card

The goal is a shared lesson, not a room full of identical laptops.

1. Put the current stage, command, expected output and branch condition on screen.
2. Invite builders to run it; narrate the saved example at the same time for observers.
3. After two minutes, stop troubleshooting individual machines. Direct builders to `solution/` or
   switch them to observer mode.
4. For the Gather checkpoint, `workshop/reference-run/gather-intersection.json` is the expected
   bounded Victorian crash evidence pack. It is a curated, non-fatal historical sample; show its
   attribution before discussing it.
5. For any local-model problem, use the saved structured result and continue with the validation
   and branch lesson. Do not wait on a runtime while the room waits with you.

Each slide/demo should answer four visible questions: What goes in? What is allowed to happen? What
comes out? Who decides whether to continue? This lets an observer follow the trust boundary just as
well as a builder.
